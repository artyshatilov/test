================================================================================
PILLAR B) GGR BUYBACK FLYWHEEL — 'buyback yield' vs HYPE (~7% of mcap/yr)
================================================================================
  Assumes: edge 2%, turnover 20x  -> GGR = 40% of TVL/yr
  Protocol take 15% of GGR; 80% of take -> buyback+burn.
  buyback$ = 12.0% of GGR = 4.80% of TVL per year.

  Annual buyback as % of FDV  ('buyback yield'):
       TVL \ FDV      $100M      $300M     $1000M     $3000M
  ----------------------------------------------------------
           $50M      2.4%       0.8%       0.2%       0.1% 
          $200M      9.6%*      3.2%       1.0%       0.3% 
          $500M     24.0%*      8.0%*      2.4%       0.8% 

  ('*' = beats HYPE's ~7%/yr buyback rate.)
  INSIGHT: buyback yield is buyback$/FDV. It is high only when FDV is
  disciplined relative to real GGR. A $3B FDV on $50M TVL gives a 0.1%
  'yield' — a meme, not a flywheel. HYPE's 7% is impressive BECAUSE fees are
  enormous vs mcap. Do not ship a 1B-supply token into thin GGR and call the
  buyback a flywheel; it only works if you keep FDV honest until volume exists.

================================================================================
PILLAR C) $HOUSE AS FIRST-LOSS BACKSTOP — senior/junior tranche waterfall
================================================================================
  Bankroll = 75% senior (stable LPs, ~10% capped coupon, protected) + 25%
  junior (staked $HOUSE, first-loss, gets residual + 8% emissions). TVL $200M, 12x.

  tranche / game                     mean   median       p5   P(loss)     worst
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  SENIOR · Roulette-like             10.0%     10.0%     10.0%       0.0%     -20.3%
  JUNIOR($HOUSE) · Roulette-like     88.1%     88.5%     -5.9%       6.1%     -92.0%
  SENIOR · Slots                     10.0%     10.0%     10.0%       0.0%      -8.7%
  JUNIOR($HOUSE) · Slots            141.0%    141.2%     46.0%       0.7%     -92.0%

  INSIGHT: the token tranche manufactures a clean two-sided product. SENIOR
  stablecoin LPs get a near-bond-like ~10% with P(loss)≈0 -> sticky, scalable,
  risk-averse capital. JUNIOR $HOUSE stakers get a LEVERED, emissions-boosted
  yield in exchange for eating the fat left tail (occasional -100% slashing).
  This finally PAYS someone for the negative skew, and ties the token to the
  core risk engine the way HLP is core to HYPE — not a bolt-on governance coin.

================================================================================
REFLEXIVITY / WRONG-WAY RISK — why the token backstop can fail when needed
================================================================================
  Nominal junior backstop: 25% of $200M = $50M.
  Stress year: pool loses 18% of TVL ($36M) AND $HOUSE falls 60%.

  Tail loss to cover:                 $    36M
  Backstop value AFTER token crash:   $    20M   (was $50M)
  Coverage ratio (buffer / loss):       0.56x   -> SENIOR HIT (backstop fails)

  INSIGHT: a token-denominated backstop is WORTH LEAST exactly when it is
  needed MOST — its price is correlated to the same volume/GGR that drives
  the loss. A 25% junior buffer that looks like $50M is only ~$20M after a
  60% drawdown, and may not cover an 18% pool loss. Mitigations: (1) size
  junior on STRESSED token value, not spot; (2) hold part of the backstop in
  stablecoins / protocol-owned reserve (not just staked token); (3) hard
  per-game payout caps so the tail can't exceed the stressed buffer. Do NOT
  market token TVL as 'real' bankroll 1:1 — discount it for reflexivity.

NET TOKEN FLOW — does the buyback outrun the emissions? (the only question)
================================================================================
  Assume $HOUSE = $0.50, 50% of emissions sold. Buyback = 12.0% of GGR.

   year      TVL      GGR   buyback$   emit(tok)     sold$    net$/yr
  -------------------------------------------------------------------
     1y     $40M   $16.0M      $1.9M       102M    $25.6M    $-23.7M
     2y    $120M   $48.0M      $5.8M       152M    $38.1M    $-32.4M
     3y    $300M  $120.0M     $14.4M       128M    $32.0M    $-17.6M
     4y    $500M  $200.0M     $24.0M       128M    $32.0M     $-8.0M

  INSIGHT: early years are net-NEGATIVE (emissions >> buyback) — this is the
  bootstrapping subsidy, and it is real dilution. The model only inverts once
  TVL/GGR scale. Be honest in the deck: years 1-2 are paid for by the token,
  the flywheel is a YEAR-3+ phenomenon, and it is conditional on hitting volume.
