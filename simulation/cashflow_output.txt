  INSIGHT: the flip date is the whole ballgame. It is LATE and reflexive —
  thin early GGR means low FDV, so emitted tokens are worth little (small $
  sold) BUT the buyback is also tiny, and you are diluting a large % of supply.

  this curve; it is the single most honest token-health metric you have.
