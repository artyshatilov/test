
veHOUSE GAUGE & BRIBE-MARKET SIMULATION

================================================================================
1) veHOUSE LOCK RATE — and how much of it is REAL vs reflexive emissions
================================================================================
  Locker rewards = REAL fee share (20% of protocol take) +
  emissions (6%/yr of supply) + bribes. Capital locks until
  veHOUSE APR compresses to ~20%. We split the lock by SOURCE.

        TVL   FDV(8x)  fee$/yr  emit$/yr  bribe$/yr %FDV lock(real) %FDV lock(total)
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
       $50M     $144M    $0.5M    $11.6M      $0.3M             2%              43%
      $200M     $576M    $2.2M    $46.2M      $1.2M             2%              43%
      $500M    $1440M    $5.4M   $115.5M      $3.0M             2%              43%

  INSIGHT: the headline 'lots of supply locked' is mostly EMISSIONS-driven —
  i.e. you lock to earn inflation, which is reflexive and evaporates when
  emissions taper. The SUSTAINABLE lock (funded by real fee share) is only a
  few % of FDV until GGR is large, because gambling fee share per $ of FDV is
  thin. veHOUSE removes float and helps the flip date, but do not mistake
  emissions-bought locks for durable demand — re-underwrite them at lower
  emissions and check the lock survives. Only the 'real' column is yours to keep.

================================================================================
2) THE BRIBE MARKET — when is bribing +EV for a game operator?
================================================================================
  Bribe efficiency: $1 of bribe directs ~$1.4 of emissions to
  the briber's pool. Directed emissions deepen the pool -> more bet capacity ->
  more volume -> more operator revenue. Operator keeps a share of GGR.

    bribe $  emissions directed  extra depth  extra volume/yr  op revenue/yr     ROI
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
      $0.1M               $0.1M        $0.1M              $3M         $0.01M    0.1x
      $0.5M               $0.7M        $0.7M             $13M         $0.06M    0.1x
      $1.0M               $1.4M        $1.4M             $25M         $0.13M    0.1x
      $2.0M               $2.8M        $2.8M             $50M         $0.25M    0.1x

  Annual bribe ROI = efficiency x turnover x edge x op_share
                   = 1.4 x 18 x 0.02 x 0.25 = 0.13x  per year.

  INSIGHT (uncomfortable): naive single-game bribing is NOT +EV here — an
  operator recoups only ~0.13x of the bribe per year, because it captures just
  edge x op_share (~0.5%) of the volume the depth enables. This is the key way a
  CASINO gauge differs from Curve: a Curve briber wants deep liquidity for peg/
  strategic reasons and LPs capture full trading fees; a casino operator captures
  a thin rake slice, so the bribe math doesn't close on fees alone. The bribe
  market only works if you (a) bundle the operator's FULL stack (frontend + dev
  + house share, raising op_share to 50-70%), (b) make capacity STRATEGIC (a game
  literally can't accept whales without directed depth), or (c) keep bribes small
  and treat veHOUSE mainly as a real-fee + governance sink. Don't model a Curve-
  sized bribe economy onto a 2% edge — the capture rate isn't there.
