
CALIBR — PREDICTION-MARKET EDGE & CALIBRATION SIMULATION
Companion to CALIBR.md §5-§6. All figures illustrative; tune CONFIG.

==============================================================================
1. REALIZED EDGE = STATED EDGE - CALIBRATION ERROR  (per $1 contract)
==============================================================================
Strategy believes p - q = +5%. We vary how wrong p is vs the truth pi.
(no frictions yet)

  market q = 0.50   stated view p = 0.55   (stated edge = +0.05)

    calib error (p-pi) |  true pi | realized edge (pi-q) | verdict
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
                 -0.03 |     0.58 |                +0.08 | edge survives
                 +0.00 |     0.55 |                +0.05 | edge survives
                 +0.02 |     0.53 |                +0.03 | edge survives
                 +0.05 |     0.50 |                +0.00 | EDGE FLIPS NEGATIVE
                 +0.08 |     0.47 |                -0.03 | EDGE FLIPS NEGATIVE

  Read: a +5c stated edge is erased by a 5-point overconfidence (p-pi=0.05),
  and goes negative beyond it. Overconfident models don't just earn less.

==============================================================================
2. WHAT'S LEFT AFTER SPREAD + RESOLUTION RISK
==============================================================================
  market q=0.50, view p=0.55, eff_spread=0.02, resolve_risk=3%

   true pi |  gross edge |  - spread |  - resolve |  net/contract
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
      0.55 |      +0.050 |    +0.030 |     +0.013 |        +0.013
      0.53 |      +0.030 |    +0.010 |     -0.006 |        -0.006
      0.51 |      +0.010 |    -0.010 |     -0.025 |        -0.025
      0.50 |      +0.000 |    -0.020 |     -0.035 |        -0.035

  If well-calibrated (pi=0.55): net ≈ +0.013/contract on 0.52 risked
  → ~+2.6% per trade, ~+10% APR at 3-mo lockup (GROSS of fails).
  Spread + resolution risk routinely halve a headline edge.

==============================================================================
3. FRACTIONAL KELLY: A TRUE EDGE COMPOUNDS, A FAKE EDGE SIZES INTO RUIN
==============================================================================
  q=0.50, sizing as if p=0.55 (believed +5%), 100 sequential bets, 1/4 Kelly

                    scenario |  true p |  stake f |  median x | P(down 50%)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
            genuine +5% edge |   0.550 |    0.025 |      1.24 |        0.0%
           half-real (+2.5%) |   0.525 |    0.025 |      1.13 |        0.1%
         no edge (efficient) |   0.500 |    0.025 |      0.97 |        0.3%
       anti-edge (overconf.) |   0.480 |    0.025 |      0.88 |        1.0%

  Same sizing logic; only calibration differs. Kelly rewards a real edge and
  punishes a believed-but-false one — sizing is only as good as your view.

==============================================================================
4. PORTFOLIO MONTE-CARLO  (many markets, a realistic mixed book)
==============================================================================
  Each market: q ~ U(0.2,0.8); the strat's view p = q + signal. We test a
  GOOD forecaster (small real edge, well-calibrated) vs an OVERCONFIDENT one.

        forecaster | mean/contract |  win rate | % markets +EV
  ----------------------------------------------------------------
   well-calibrated |       +0.0297 |     55.0% |         88.1%
     overconfident |       -0.0082 |     51.2% |         55.2%

  Same signals, same selection rule. A 4-point calibration bias turns a
  profitable book into a losing one — even though the strategy 'looks' identical.

==============================================================================
TAKEAWAY: realized edge = calibration, not confidence. Spread + resolution
risk halve headline edges, and Kelly sizing amplifies whichever you actually
have. The product's job is to MEASURE this honestly, not to promise alpha.
==============================================================================
